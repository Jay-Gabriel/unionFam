Graffico Office - the Graffico studio in 3D

An interactive 3D studio, explored in first person from the browser: walk between the two workstations, tune a radio that plays real stations, write HTML and CSS on a screen that renders the site live, read the project board and the awards shelf, sit in the lounge - and, if you can’t resist, pull the plug out of the wall socket.

Built in Blender with baked lighting and rendered in the browser with React Three Fiber. Keyboard and mouse: WASD to walk, mouse to look around, E to interact with the objects that light up.

Made by Graffico, the studio this office belongs to.
